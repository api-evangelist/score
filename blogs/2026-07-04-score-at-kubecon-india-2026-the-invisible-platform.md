At KubeCon India 2026, Mumshad Mannambeth and I (Abhinav Sharma) gave a talk called “The Death of the YAML-Engineer.” The title was provocative on purpose. We weren’t announcing a job elimination - we were arguing for a promotion. The average developer deploying to Kubernetes today spends more time on infrastructure YAML than on their application.
